Witness & Humanitarian Travel

Our most ethically robust and fastest-growing category: recent history, told by the people and communities who lived through it.

Sites we work with

  • Srebrenica (Bosnia & Herzegovina) — The Memorial Centre and Cemetery, central to understanding Europe's most recent genocide.
  • Mostar (Bosnia & Herzegovina) — A war-scarred city and the rebuilt Old Bridge — a powerful, visual testimony to recovery.
  • Sarajevo Siege Route (Bosnia & Herzegovina) — The Tunnel of Hope, the Olympic sites, and the city's wartime cemeteries.
  • Robben Island (South Africa) — Nelson Mandela's prison, and living history told by former-prisoner guides.
  • Belfast Murals & the Troubles (Northern Ireland) — Black cab tours through the Falls Road and Shankill, and Crumlin Road Gaol.

Who this journey is for

Travellers who want to understand a conflict within living memory, not just read about it. Bosnia in particular remains almost entirely unserved by UK specialists — it's accessible, affordable, and profound.

Our approach here

Itineraries here are built in consultation with local memorial organisations, and we prioritise locally-owned guides and accommodation so your spending benefits the communities you're visiting.
