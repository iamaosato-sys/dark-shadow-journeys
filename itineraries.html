Sample Itineraries

Five starting points. Every one is a starting point, not a fixed package.

Every itinerary below is fully bespoke to your dates, budget and group size. Prices are per person, based on a small group of 4–6 unless noted.

Normandy Witnessed

£2,800–£3,500pp

5 days, France — for WWII anniversary pilgrims and family veterans.

Day 1Caen Memorial Museum — context-setting for the Battle of Normandy
Day 2American Cemetery, Omaha Beach and Pointe du Hoc
Day 3British and Canadian sectors: Gold, Juno, Sword; Bayeux War Cemetery and Tapestry
Day 4Arromanches 360° cinema; the Longues-sur-Mer battery; Utah Beach
Day 5Private archive research at the Caen Memorial for family veteran records

Sarajevo Survivor

£2,200–£2,800pp

7 days, Bosnia & Herzegovina — our signature journey, and Europe's most recent genocide understood through those who lived it.

Day 1Arrive Sarajevo; neighbourhood walk; dinner in a local home
Day 2War Childhood Museum; Sniper Alley; the Tunnel of Hope
Day 3Srebrenica — the Memorial Centre and Cemetery (a full day; emotionally intense)
Day 4Mostar; the Old Bridge; a war-damage walking tour with a local guide
Day 5Blagaj and the Kravice Waterfalls — a decompression day
Day 6Sarajevo food market; a meeting with a local NGO; free time
Day 7Depart

Auschwitz & Kraków: Bearing Witness

£1,600–£2,200pp

4 days, Poland — educational, and deeply ethical by design.

Day 1Arrive Kraków; Jewish Quarter walk; Schindler's Factory Museum
Day 2Auschwitz I (licensed guided tour, morning); Birkenau (self-reflective walk, afternoon)
Day 3Kazimierz market; a meeting with a local Jewish community historian; optional Wieliczka Salt Mine
Day 4Depart

Fukushima: The Zone

£4,500–£6,500pp

8 days, Japan — for nuclear history and science enthusiasts, as Japan continues to expand access.

Day 1Tokyo arrival
Day 2Hiroshima Peace Memorial Museum
Day 3Travel to the Fukushima region
Day 4Licensed tour of open exclusion-zone areas; J-Village; Futaba Town
Day 5Decontamination facility visit and briefing
Day 6Travel to Kyoto
Day 7Kyoto and debrief
Day 8Depart

Belfast: Murals & Memory

£800–£1,200pp

3 days, Northern Ireland — our best-value introductory journey. UK mainland flight, no passport required.

Day 1Black cab tour of the Falls Road and Shankill murals with a local driver-guide from both communities
Day 2Titanic Belfast; the Peace Wall; Crumlin Road Gaol tour
Day 3Free time; depart
