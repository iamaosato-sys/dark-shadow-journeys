Your Data

Privacy & cookies

Last updated 18 November 2026. Plain English: we collect as little as possible, and analytics only runs if you say yes.

Who we are

Dark Shadow Journeys is run by Ao Sato, trading as Dark Shadow Journeys, based on the Fylde Coast, Lancashire, United Kingdom. We are the data controller for personal data processed through this website. Contact: hello@darkshadowjourneys.com.

What we collect

Enquiries. The contact form does not send anything to this website. Pressing Send opens your own email app; if you send that email we receive your name, email address and whatever you write about your trip. We use it only to reply and plan your journey.

Analytics (only if you accept). If you click “Accept” on the cookie banner we use Google Analytics 4 to collect pseudonymous usage data such as pages viewed, approximate location (country/city), device and browser type. If you reject or ignore the banner, Google Analytics does not load and sets no cookies.

Hosting logs. Our hosting provider processes basic technical data (such as IP address and browser) to deliver and secure the site.

We do not take payments on this website and we do not use advertising cookies.

Lawful basis

  • Replying to enquiries: steps you ask us to take before a possible contract, and our legitimate interest in answering you (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b) and (f)).
  • Analytics: your consent (Article 6(1)(a)). You can change your mind any time with “Cookie settings” in the footer.
  • Hosting and security: our legitimate interest in running a safe, working website (Article 6(1)(f)).

Who we share data with

  • Google Ireland Ltd / Google LLC — analytics, only with your consent.
  • Our website host (GitHub Pages / Vercel) — hosting.
  • Our email provider — to receive and answer enquiries.
  • If you book, the travel suppliers and ATOL-holding partner needed to deliver your trip; we will tell you who they are before you book.

Some providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, they rely on safeguards recognised under UK law, such as the UK–US data bridge or the International Data Transfer Addendum.

How long we keep it

Enquiry emails: up to 2 years after our last contact, unless you book (booking records are kept for 6 years for tax and legal reasons). Google Analytics data: 14 months.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, restrict or object to its use, move it, or withdraw consent. Email hello@darkshadowjourneys.com and we will reply within one month. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

This site is for adults planning travel. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.
